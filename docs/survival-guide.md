# classic reborn survival guide

## start a survival world

from the main menu, choose **singleplayer**, set the mode to **survival**, and leave **flat world** off for a start with natural terrain. open chat with `t` and enter `/difficulty` to see your current setting. use `/difficulty easy` for a first night with hostile mobs, or `/difficulty peaceful` to practice gathering and building without natural hostile spawns.

| default control | action |
| --- | --- |
| `w`, `a`, `s`, `d` and `space` | move and jump |
| `shift` | run |
| hold left mouse | mine the block under the crosshair |
| left mouse | attack a mob |
| right mouse | place a block, interact, or use the selected item |
| `e` | open inventory and the 2×2 crafting grid |
| `1` to `9` or mouse wheel | select a hotbar slot |

## collect wood and get through the first night

1. **collect logs.** hold left mouse on a tree trunk, then walk over the dropped logs. aim for at least three logs for your first tools. gather extra wood or dirt to close up your shelter.
2. **make a workbench.** put one log in the inventory crafting grid and take the four planks from the output. fill the 2×2 grid with those planks to make a workbench. put it on your hotbar, place it, then right-click it for a 3×3 grid.
3. **make a pickaxe.** stack two planks vertically to make four sticks. at the workbench, put three planks across the top row and two sticks down the center below them. use the wooden pickaxe on exposed rock to gather cobblestone. save three pieces for a stone pickaxe and two for a stone sword. for the sword, stack two pieces of cobblestone above one stick. the mined cobblestone stack may be labeled **stone** in the inventory.
4. **get light and healing.** mine exposed coal ore. put one coal above one stick to make four torches. pick up apples that drop from leaves, or gather raw porkchops from pigs and raw beef from cows. right-click with food selected when you have missing hearts. food restores health directly.
5. **close up before dark.** make a small shelter near a landmark. add a roof to keep climbing spiders out, light the inside, and close the entrance with a block when you go in. if you run out of daylight, seal a shallow hillside alcove first and finish crafting inside. check the area before leaving in the morning. creepers and husks can still be outside.

the recipe book in the inventory or workbench can fill the grid when you have the ingredients. select **all recipes** to see what you are missing. the inventory book shows recipes that fit the 2×2 grid, so open the workbench book for tools and other larger recipes.

## fight common mobs

keep your sword, healing food, torches, and spare blocks on the hotbar. pick fights on level ground with a clear retreat path. if several mobs join the fight or your hearts drop low, get behind solid cover and heal.

| mob | what to watch for | how to fight it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| zombie | melee attacks, smaller variants that are easy to miss, and nearby zombies joining a fight on normal and above | face the zombie and step back between hits. check behind you before retreating. open sunlight can burn ordinary zombies, but helmets and water can protect them. desert husks are immune to sunlight. |
| skeleton | arrows across open ground and shorter breaks between shots on hard and above | use a wall or hillside to break its line of sight. change angles under cover, then close in when you have a short approach. pick up landed arrows once the area is clear. |
| creeper | hissing signals a fuse that can destroy blocks as well as hurt you | back away immediately and break its line of sight. use a bow when you have one, or land a hit and back away before moving in again. lead it away from your base before fighting. |
| spider | lunges and wall climbing. an unprovoked spider can be neutral in bright areas | pass it by in daylight if it leaves you alone. if it attacks, move to open ground so you can see the lunge. use a roofed shelter if you need to retreat. |
| enderman | looking directly at its eyes can provoke it, and it can teleport | keep your crosshair low as you pass. if it turns hostile, break line of sight and retreat. water can drive it away. |

once you have spare resources, make armor and equip it in the inventory armor slots. the workbench has early armor recipes using planks or cobblestone. when you find iron ore, use the raw iron directly in iron tool and armor recipes. a bow uses three sticks and three string, which you can collect from spiders. hold right mouse to draw the bow, then release to shoot an arrow from your inventory.

## spot ambushes and fight special mobs

### zombie igniter

- **spot it.** look for a zombie, husk, or frostbite holding flint and steel. igniters can spawn naturally on very hard and extreme. keep your distance even if it has yet to swing. it can set you on fire from about two and a half blocks away if it has a clear view.
- **if it closes in.** back around a solid corner to cut its line of sight, then use a bow from farther away. if you catch fire, get into nearby water to put it out and heal once you have room. on extreme, an igniter can start fires at the wooden walls of an occupied generated house. if the wall catches fire, leave by a clear exit and put distance between you and the igniter before turning to fight.

### zombie builder

- **spot it.** look for a zombie carrying a gray block, or watch for one placing steps or breaking blocks in its way. builders generate inside cobblestone towers, with iron-helmeted residents on the lower and upper floors. check both floors before using a tower as shelter. builders can also spawn outdoors on very hard and extreme.
- **if it closes in.** leave your ledge by a safe route as soon as you see steps going up. use a bow across open ground, or land a sword hit and back off before it reaches melee range. on extreme, a builder can tear the head off a turret and shoot it. if you see it carrying a turret head, get behind solid cover and move between shots.

### skeleton trap

- **spot it.** on extreme, a skeleton trap can make a death sound and collapse into a pile of bones as you approach. stop when you see it fall. step back from the pile and go around it. getting too close can pin you in place and open a pit beneath you where the ground allows it.
- **if it grabs you.** look at the pile and left-click each visible piece individually, including the skull, torso, arms, and legs. breaking the last piece releases you. if a pit opened, use spare blocks to climb out. you can also break the pieces from beyond the trigger area if your crosshair can reach them. arrows work against the trap while it is standing, and the scattered pile is cleared by breaking its pieces.

### wild iron golem

- **spot it.** a wild iron golem can spawn near inhabited village homes and ambush you. look for the tall iron figure before rounding a house corner or walking into an open yard.
- **if it closes in.** get around a solid corner or through a narrow covered passage. its swing can knock you up and back, so stay clear of ledges and holes. if you decide to fight, use a bow from open ground with space to retreat.

### ironmock

ironmocks can generate in the side galleries of desert temples, called desert pyramids in the game. listen for anvil sounds and check the galleries before exploring deeper. if one rushes you, back out into open ground and keep it beyond melee range.

## choose a difficulty

the table describes singleplayer. enter `/difficulty` followed by the setting shown below to change it.

| setting | what changes | how to prepare |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `peaceful` | natural hostile spawning is disabled. lava and drowning are still hazards. | learn the recipes and practice building a shelter. |
| `easy` | fewer natural hostiles than normal. zombie hits, skeleton arrows, and spider hits deal roughly half their normal damage. | make a stone sword and practice dealing with one mob at a time. |
| `normal` | the default. zombies can alert nearby zombies when hit, and undead can spawn with armor. | check the area before taking the first swing and leave room to retreat. |
| `hard` | more natural hostiles, faster zombies, more frequent undead gear, and shorter pauses between skeleton shots. the usual zombie, skeleton, and spider attacks hit about 50% harder than on normal. | equip armor and bring healing before exploring caves or going out at night. |
| `very_hard` | zombie hits and skeleton arrows can deal double normal damage. igniters and builders enter the normal hostile spawn rotation. | use ranged attacks against igniters and plan a way down from any elevated position. |
| `extreme` | the highest natural hostile spawn cap. igniters are more common and can attack occupied generated houses. skeleton traps can collapse into a pile of bones, then open a pit and grab you when you approach. | shelter behind stone, keep an escape route, and give suspicious bone piles a wide berth. |

## earn and spend minecoins

killing mobs such as zombies, skeletons, spiders, and creepers earns minecoins. when the game credits you with the kill, the coins go straight into your balance. check it in the marketplace before you buy. dying clears your balance under the default inventory rules, so spend coins on supplies you can use before a risky trip.

in singleplayer survival, press `esc` and choose **marketplace (parody)**. use **previous** and **next** to browse, then click an offer to buy it. leave room in your inventory for the purchase.

| offer | cost | what it gives you |
| --- | --- | --- |
| apple | 4 minecoins | food to restore missing health |
| 5 arrows | 5 minecoins | ammunition for fighting from a distance |
| bow | 20 minecoins | a ranged weapon you can buy before you have enough string |
| defender | 30 minecoins | an ally that spawns on safe ground nearby |

the marketplace also sells tools, weapons, armor, and items such as the grappling hook. you can spend the same minecoins on local trades by right-clicking a friendly villager. check the offer and price before buying.

## protect your base with defenders and turrets

### hire and equip a defender

1. **hire one near your base.** stand on open, dry ground and buy a **defender** for 30 minecoins in the marketplace. it spawns on safe ground nearby with a wooden sword.
2. **give it gear.** get close and right-click the defender to open its equipment screen. left-click a sword or armor piece in your inventory to equip it, or drag it to the matching slot. the sword goes in **weapon**. click equipped gear to take it back when your inventory has room.
3. **use it to cover your retreat.** a defender follows you and fights nearby threats in melee. give it a stone sword and spare armor, then lead attackers toward a clear area near your entrance. use a turret for coverage at a fixed position while you explore.

### craft, place, and load a crossbow turret

1. **craft the turret at a workbench.** put a bow in the top center, one raw iron between two planks in the middle row, and three cobblestone across the bottom.
2. **place it where it can see the approach.** select the turret and right-click the top of a solid block with clear space above it. position it to cover the approach to your entrance so walls and corners leave a clear shot. keep enough room to reach the turret safely for loading.
3. **load it.** craft four crossbow bolts by placing one raw iron above one stick. select the bolts in your hotbar and right-click the turret. it loads as many bolts as it can from the selected stack, up to 256 in a standard crossbow turret. it fires automatically at eligible mobs when it has a clear shot. keep a spare stack by the entrance. other turret types may take arrows, bullets, or their own ammunition.
4. **check or repair it.** right-click the turret with a **turret control unit** selected to check ammunition, toggle firing, or review its targets. the workbench recipe book shows the control unit and repair kit recipes. use a **turret repair kit** on a damaged turret to restore 10 health. keep the target list focused on the threats you want it to shoot.

### recover a stolen turret head

on extreme, repeated clicks from a builder at your turret mean it is trying to tear off the head. knock it away from the turret or take it down from cover before it finishes. once the head comes off, the turret stops firing and the builder takes its loaded ammunition.

1. **get behind solid cover.** the builder can shoot the stolen ammunition at you. move between shots and use your bow from a position with room to retreat.
2. **defeat the builder and collect the recovered turret head.** it drops the head and any stolen ammunition it has left.
3. **reattach and reload.** select the recovered turret head and right-click the surviving turret base. then select its ammunition and right-click again to reload. check that firing is enabled before relying on it again.

## common survival mistakes

- **spending most of your minecoins on food.** carry enough healing for the trip, then save coins for a weapon, armor, or ammunition. for everyday healing, gather apples or hunt pigs and cows.
- **taking every fight, even at low health.** break line of sight and heal behind cover. let a defender or turret handle nearby threats while you recover, and skip fights that would cut off your escape.
- **entering a cave unprepared.** caves can be dark enough for hostile mobs even during the day. from normal onward, a fight with one zombie can draw others. on hard and above, expect more hostiles, harder hits, and quicker follow-up shots from skeletons. on extreme, give suspicious bone piles a wide berth. bring a sword, pickaxe, torches, healing food, spare blocks, and armor. mark your exit as you descend and turn back while you still have light and healing for the return trip.
- **forgetting to reload your defenses.** check turret ammunition after a fight and keep backup bolts at the base. on extreme, cover a turret with a defender or a second firing angle so a builder has less time to reach it.
- **provoking or killing villagers.** put your weapon away around villagers and use right-click to trade. every three hits on a villager cost 2 reputation, and a player-credited kill costs 7. nearby villagers temporarily refuse to trade after a kill. at `-100` reputation, villagers can turn hostile toward you in survival.

## explore and recover from a death

- **mark the way out.** place torches on the same side as you go deeper into a cave. keep them on the opposite side on your way back. bring spare blocks to bridge a small gap or close off a passage.
- **watch the ground and water.** approach ledges from solid footing and dig a staircase when going down so you can climb back out. look for lava and leave enough air to surface when swimming.
- **keep a spare kit.** a chest takes eight planks around the edge of the 3×3 grid. leave a spare pickaxe, sword, food, and torches near spawn so you can re-equip after a death.
- **recover drops when the route is safe.** choose **respawn** on the game over screen. by default, death drops your inventory and equipped armor where you died. drops expire after about five minutes of active ticking. bring your spare gear if the same mobs are still there.
